Common questions

Can you add or swap exhibits after we’ve sent the list?

Yes. Send changes as they come and we’ll confirm any effect on the timeline.

I’m not sure what I need yet. Can I still reach out?

Yes. Share your question or explain your situation and we’ll guide you on the next steps, with no obligation.

Can you handle a large or complicated set?

Yes. Send what you have and we’ll build the plan around it.

How many copies should I order?

Most trials need a set for you, the judge, opposing counsel and the witness stand. We’ll confirm the count with you before we start.

What formats do you deliver?

Binders, folders or digital files, or a mix. Tell us how your team will work in the courtroom.
